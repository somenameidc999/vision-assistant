# Camera-only demo interface

Date: 2026-11-06.

## Scope

Implement the user's live-camera demo and simplified controls. Prerecorded video remains an explicit development fixture. No iPhone was available for this change.

## Implementation

- Idle: one centered camera icon button. Long-press opens Connection settings.
- Active: icon-only mute, stop speaking, switch camera, and end controls with accessibility labels.
- Remove visible frame counts, sampling captions, and request metrics. Retain internal sampling and budget accounting.
- Rear and front camera inputs share one capture session. Camera switching interrupts the pending answer, clears visual history, and requires a fresh frame.
- Keep the previous camera when a replacement input cannot be added. Reject old-source callbacks and samples captured before switching.
- Use rotation coordinators for preview and capture. Both views remain unmirrored.
- Compile the video source and player only in Debug Simulator builds. Require `--dev-prerecorded-scene`. Camera failures never enable the fixture.

## Limits

No physical camera was tested. Front/rear capture, rotation, camera-switch recovery, and capture shutdown still require an iPhone. The Simulator checks do not prove live microphone input, audible output heard by a person, voice interruption, or echo control. No new claim is made about those acceptance targets.
