# Demo media

The coffee photograph is by Rachel Michetti. The photographer released the photograph under CC0.

- [Source image, pinned scikit-image v0.19.0](https://github.com/scikit-image/scikit-image/blob/v0.19.0/skimage/data/coffee.png)

Checked: 2026-11-06.

`coffee.jpg` is a compressed copy. `demo-scene.mp4` animates the photograph on a canvas and changes a generated table label.
The photograph moves; this does not represent a real cup moving through a live scene.
The video is silent and clearly labeled as prerecorded. The generated overlays are project-created test material.
Regenerate with `scripts/make_fixtures.py`. No personal camera capture is included.
